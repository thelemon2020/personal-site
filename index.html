Bio

I'm a software consultant, who focuses on high quality deliverables through communication and collaboration.

Off hours, I'm developing CollectShine a smart shelf system for physical media collectors

I speak at various conferences and meetups. I also run a PHP meetup called GPUG. When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably flipping through records.
